Insecure Features: Attack Classes

Evaluation

Evaluation Summary #

We evaluated 28 popular PDF applications on Windows, macOS, and Linux, and the PDF viewers built into web browsers. 26 of them are vulnerable to at least one attack. The attack classes are described on the attack classes page; the full results and their discussion are in Section VII of our paper.
